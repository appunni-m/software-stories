A map you can open
The introduction is curated from this project’s source. Selecting a part or asking a question opens a narrated detail. Finish it before exploring further. Back restores your previous explanation and playback position, including while work is loading.
Each claim links to an immutable source snapshot. Exact quotes are checked; interpretation can still be wrong, and inferences are labeled.
Flow, sequence, branch, state, memory, comparison, and code views share one renderer. The same timeline powers playback and MP4 export. New explanations choose the visual form for the behavior being explained. Spoken cues guide the visual timeline; older chapters use estimated timing. Source reading highlights are explanatory guides, not recorded execution traces.
