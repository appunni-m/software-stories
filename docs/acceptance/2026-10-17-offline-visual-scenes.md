# Offline visual scenes and scene audio — 17 October 2026

## Delivered behavior

The offline exporter now renders validated HTML scene compositions, with local
source links and manual previous/next navigation. Section notes and transcripts
start collapsed. Graph scenes retain their disclosed source-outline fallback.
No scripts, automatic scene advance or cross-track playback controller are
included. See [export instructions and limits](../OFFLINE_EXPORT.md).

The player and exporter share narration storage scopes. Aligned multi-claim
stories export separately cached scene clips with their exact reviewed text;
custom whole-section scripts and legacy summaries stay whole. Missing clips
produce explicit partial/transcript-only coverage. Export reads the existing
native review/cache commands and never generates speech. Schema 4 records each
scene's identity, transcript hash, audio hash and cache fingerprint.

Review also exposed a privacy gap: presentation titles and values had escaped
the older export text filter. They now use the same credential/path filtering.
Omitting source excerpts also removes declared source-backed visual values,
with a visible omission note. Labels, illustrative examples and citation paths
remain. Audio is not rewritten by text filtering; users must listen before
sharing. The filters do not establish that every possible secret is removed.
